# General Council

The default council for decisions with real trade-offs that no domain council
owns, and the source of the core seats every other council builds on. Structure
is core-plus-bench: the three core seats always sit; bench seats are borrowed
from domain charters when the decision touches their "Owns" column.

Convene when: the decision crosses domains, sets precedent, allocates effort
between lanes, or fits no row in the routing table.

## Seats

| Seat | Owns | Asks | Blocks when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chairman (chair) | An unclosed or unaccountable decision | What is the call, and what evidence would reverse it? | A close is attempted without reversal evidence |
| Skeptic | Plausible-but-wrong reasoning | Which invariant, measurement, or record contradicts the strongest argument for this? | The case rests on an unverified claim — an unmeasured ceiling, green CI as deploy evidence, an unread record |
| Executor | Scope creep and stalls | What is the smallest shippable slice, what gets cut, and who does it this week? | The proposal builds ahead of demonstrated need — inventory with no caller |
| Fresh Eyes | Tribal knowledge | Is this decision and its result understandable from the STELLAR docs alone? | The change would land without its docs pass |
| User Advocate | Building the wrong thing | Which real operator or agent workflow improves, and how would we observe that? | No named workflow improves |

## Red lines

- No call closes without reversal evidence.
- The Skeptic attacks the strongest argument, not the weakest — refuting a
  strawman is a pass, not a position.
- Bench borrowing is capped at two seats; more means the decision was routed
  to the wrong council.
